# FinBridge Win11 Migration — End User Feedback: Action Ranking
Date: 2026-08-12  
Analyst: DWP Endpoint Engineering  
Source: 50 post-migration comments, FinBridge staff

---

## Ranking Methodology
Themes were weighted by **impact first, volume second**.  
A single Blocker outranks any number of Minor or Friction issues.  
Where multiple themes share the same severity (all Blockers), volume and persistence broke the tie.

---

## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

---

### Rank 1 — Account Lockout / AVD Sign-in Failure
**Count:** 7 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**  
Highest volume among all Blocker themes. Users are completely unable to work — some locked out for 2+ hours, some experiencing repeat lockouts multiple times in the same week, and at least one incident occurred immediately before a client call. This is the broadest work-stoppage issue in the dataset.

**Representative quotes:**
- *"Cannot log in to AVD at all since this morning. Tried 3 times. Urgent."*
- *"Account locked again, this is the third time this week."*

**Manager summary:**  
Seven FinBridge staff are experiencing total work stoppages due to account lockouts and AVD sign-in failures, with repeat incidents indicating the root cause has not yet been resolved.

---

### Rank 2 — Floor 3 Printer Not Mapping
**Count:** 6 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**  
Second highest volume Blocker. Critically, the comments span multiple days and show escalating frustration — staff have given up waiting and are walking to another floor to print. This is a team-wide failure that is blocking client-facing document work, and the issue predates today, meaning it has already had extended dwell time without resolution.

**Representative quotes:**
- *"Printer on floor 3 – whole team can't print client docs."*
- *"Team has given up and is walking to floor 2 to print."*

**Manager summary:**  
The floor 3 printer mapping failure is a persistent, team-wide Blocker now in at least its third day, actively affecting client document production with no resolution in sight.

---

### Rank 3 — OneDrive Files Missing or Not Syncing
**Count:** 4 comments | **Severity:** Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**  
Tied on volume with VPN instability (also 4 comments, also Blocker), but ranked above VPN due to **data integrity risk**. Users report files as missing — not just inaccessible — and at least two comments reference time-critical deliverables (a meeting and a Q1 report due end of day). Perceived data loss carries reputational and compliance risk beyond a connectivity drop.

**Representative quotes:**
- *"My OneDrive files are missing! This is urgent, need them for a meeting."*
- *"Missing files in OneDrive – checked three times, still not there."*

**Manager summary:**  
Four staff are reporting OneDrive files as missing post-migration, with at least two citing immediate deadline impact — this carries data integrity and compliance risk that must be ruled out before end of business today.
