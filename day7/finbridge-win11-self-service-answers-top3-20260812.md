# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Self-Service Answers: Top 3 Issues
Date: 2026-08-12
Audience: FinBridge business users

---

## Issue 1: Cannot access the credentials vault

**Try these steps first:**
1. Sign out of the vault completely, close your browser, reopen it, and sign back in.
2. If that does not work, try opening the vault in a different browser (e.g. if you normally use Edge, try Chrome, or vice versa).

**Still not working? Contact the service desk.**
Please have the following ready so we can help you faster:
- Your name and team
- The date and time it first stopped working
- Any error message on screen (a photo on your phone is fine)

---

## Issue 2: Locked out of the admin console

**Try these steps first:**
1. Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again — accounts can lock temporarily after too many attempts and unlock on their own.
2. Try from a different device if you have one available.

**Still locked out? Contact the service desk.**
Please have the following ready so we can help you faster:
- Your username
- What you were trying to do when it happened
- The exact error message shown on screen

---

## Issue 3: Cannot connect to your test virtual machine

**Try these steps first:**
1. Make sure you are connected to the VPN — open the VPN app, check it shows as connected, and try again.
2. Close the remote connection window fully, wait 30 seconds, and reopen it.

**Still cannot connect? Contact the service desk.**
Please have the following ready so we can help you faster:
- The name of the virtual machine you are trying to reach
- Whether your VPN shows as connected (yes or no)
- Any error message on screen
