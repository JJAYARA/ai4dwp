# All-Staff Notice — FinBridge Win11 Migration: Known Issues Update
Date: 2026-08-12
From: DWP Endpoint Engineering
To: All FinBridge Staff

---

We are aware that some colleagues are experiencing difficulty with three areas following the recent Windows 11 migration: **accessing the credentials vault**, **signing in to the admin console**, and **connecting to test virtual machines**. We are actively investigating all three and working to resolve them as quickly as possible.

While we work on a fix, please try the following before raising a ticket:

- **Credentials vault:** Sign out fully, close your browser, and sign back in. If that fails, try a different browser.
- **Admin console:** Wait 15 minutes and try again. If available, try from a different device.
- **Test VMs:** Check your VPN shows as connected, then close and reopen the connection.

If the above steps do not help, please contact the service desk with your username, the time the issue started, and any error message on screen.

We will share a further update by end of business today. Thank you for your patience — we know this is frustrating and we are on it.

— DWP Endpoint Engineering
