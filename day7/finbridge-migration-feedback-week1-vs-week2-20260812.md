## NEW Theme Identified in Week 2

---

### Excel Crashes on Large Files — NEW
**Week 2 count:** 9 (comments 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37) | **Severity: Blocker**

This theme did not appear in week 1 at all. It emerged in week 2 as the single largest complaint by volume. Users report Excel crashing or freezing when opening large files (one user specifies files over 10MB), causing loss of unsaved work. The finance team appears disproportionately affected given references to budget spreadsheets.

- *"Excel keeps crashing on our biggest budget spreadsheet, happened 3 times today."*
- *"Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."*
- *"Excel crashing has become a real productivity problem for finance team."*

**Note:** With 9 comments in a single week and explicit escalation language ("please prioritise", "urgently"), this is the highest-priority open issue heading into week 3.
