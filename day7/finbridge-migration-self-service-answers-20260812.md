# FinBridge Win11 Migration — Self-Service Answers
Date: 2026-08-12

---

## 1. Locked out of your account or can't sign in to your desktop?

Wait 15 minutes before trying again — repeated wrong attempts will keep your account locked longer. When you're ready to try, make sure Caps Lock is off, then sign in as normal.

If you need access urgently and cannot wait, contact the Service Desk straight away.

**Contact the Service Desk if:** you are still locked out after 15 minutes, or this has happened more than once this week.  
**Tell them:** your name, your username, the time it happened, and whether you saw an error message — this helps them unlock your account immediately.

---

## 2. Printer on floor 3 not working?

Try this first: log off your computer completely and log back in — this refreshes your printer connections automatically.

If the printer still does not appear after logging back in, do not keep retrying. Walk to the floor 2 printer for anything urgent while IT resolves this.

**Contact the Service Desk if:** the printer is still missing after you log back in.  
**Tell them:** your name, your floor, your computer name (shown at the bottom-left of your screen or on a sticker on the device), and roughly when the problem started.

---

## 3. Files missing from OneDrive?

First, open OneDrive in your web browser at [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.com) and sign in with your work account — your files may still be there even if they are not visible on your desktop yet. Check the Recycle Bin inside OneDrive in case files were accidentally removed.

**Contact the Service Desk immediately if:** you cannot find your files online either, or you need them for a deadline today.  
**Tell them:** your name, the names of the missing files or folders, roughly when you last saw them, and your deadline — this lets IT prioritise data recovery for you.
