# Floor 6 computer takes a long time to log on

If your computer takes a long time to log on or feels slow after you reach the desktop, your files and data are safe. The IT team is checking this, and you do not need to change any computer options.

What you can do:
1. Save any work you have open.
2. Log off your computer.
3. Log on again.
4. If it is still slow, wait 10 minutes and try again.
5. If it is still not better, turn your computer off and on once.

If the problem stops after you log on again or restart, keep working as usual.

If it does not get better, contact the service desk.

Please include these details in your request:
- The time the slow log on started.
- Whether you could log on at all.
- Whether log on was slow every time or only once.
- Whether logging off and back on helped.
- Whether restarting helped.
- The name shown on your computer, if you can see it.
- Any message you saw on the screen.

Contact the service desk if the problem keeps happening, gets worse, or stops you from working.
