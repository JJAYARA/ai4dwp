# L1 Self-Service: New Work Computer Setup Not Finishing

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 11/08/2026  
Status : Draft

If your new work computer setup does not finish, your data is safe. This issue affects setup only and does not remove or damage your files.

## What you can do now

1. Keep the computer turned on and connected to the internet.
2. Restart the computer once.
3. Try the setup again.
4. If it still does not complete, wait 15 minutes and try one more time.
5. If it does not work again, stop and contact the Service Desk.

## What not to do

- Do not keep trying many times in a row.
- Do not erase the computer yourself.
- Do not change your work sign-in settings unless the Service Desk asks you to.

## Why this can happen (simple explanation)

Sometimes a computer still has old company setup details from earlier use. When those old details are still attached, new setup can be blocked until support removes them.

## Contact the service desk if...

Contact the Service Desk if setup still does not finish after one restart and two more tries (including the 15-minute wait), or if you see the same message on screen again.

## Details to include in your ticket

- Your full name
- Best contact number
- Computer name (if shown)
- Computer ID number (from sticker or box)
- Date and time the setup failed
- A photo or screen picture of the message shown
- Whether you restarted and tried again
- Your location (office/home)

The Service Desk will guide the next steps and confirm when setup is complete.
