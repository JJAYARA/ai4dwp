Title: Black Screen After Sign-In - Quick Help
Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

If your work screen goes black after you sign in, do not worry. Your files and emails are safe.

This problem is usually temporary and can often be fixed by signing out and signing in again.

What you can do now:

1. Sign out of your work screen.
Expected result: you return to the sign-in page.

2. Wait 60 seconds.
Expected result: any frozen connection has time to clear.

3. Sign in again.
Expected result: your main work screen opens normally.

4. If the screen is still black, sign out again.
Expected result: your sign-in is fully closed.

5. Wait 2 minutes.
Expected result: the system has time to reset in the background.

6. Restart your computer.
Expected result: your device starts fresh.

7. Sign in to your work screen one more time.
Expected result: your main work screen opens and stays usable.

8. If it still does not work, stop trying again and contact the Service Desk.
Expected result: support can help quickly and avoid temporary account blocks.

Your data is safe:
- Your files are not deleted by this issue.
- Your email and documents remain in your account.
- Support can restore access without losing your work.

Contact the Service Desk if:
- the screen is still black after the steps above,
- you keep getting signed out,
- you cannot sign in after restarting.

Please include these details in your request:
- Your full name
- Your team/department
- Your phone number
- Your location (office/home)
- Computer type (laptop/desktop)
- Approximate time the issue started
- What you saw (for example: black screen, signed out message, error message)
- What steps you already tried
- Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes
- Picture of any message on screen (if possible)
